# 🚀 FeedIntel

### *Smart Feedback. Smarter Institutions.*

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement

Institutions regularly collect student and faculty feedback, but most of it remains underutilized.

* Feedback is scattered across forms and spreadsheets
* Manual analysis delays decision-making
* No structured trend tracking or sentiment insights
* Data privacy risks with third‑party platforms

As a result, critical academic decisions are reactive instead of data-driven.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

**FeedIntel** is a **self-hosted institutional feedback intelligence platform** that transforms raw feedback into **AI-powered actionable insights**.

It enables institutions to securely collect, analyze, and interpret feedback for smarter academic and administrative decisions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📝 **Smart Survey Builder** – Structured & open-ended feedback collection
* 🔒 **Self-Hosted & Privacy-First** – Full institutional data ownership
* 🤖 **AI Analytics Engine** – Sentiment analysis, trend tracking, keyword insights
* 📊 **Interactive Dashboards** – Visual analytics for quick understanding
* 🚨 **Predictive Alerts** – Early detection of declining satisfaction
* 👥 **Role-Based Access** – Admin, Faculty, HOD level controls
* 🕒 **Semester-wise Comparison** – Performance trends over time

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Planned)

**Frontend:** React / Next.js
**Backend:** Node.js + Express
**Database:** MongoDB
**AI Layer:** NLP & LLM APIs
**Deployment:** Docker-based Self-Hosting (VPS / Institutional Servers)
